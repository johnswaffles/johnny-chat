# Ashen Outrider rider and horse artwork

Nine original built-in image-generation sheets establish the Ashen Outrider: an olive-tan, dark-haired rider in a rust-red headband, worn brown leather, charcoal wool and a separate taupe fur cloak; and a full-size smoke-brown shaggy horse with black mane/tail, rough leather tack and a russet saddlecloth. The rider's spear, small wooden shield and reins remain runtime equipment. Saddle and blanket are a separate part from the breathing barrel.

All nine PNGs have genuine RGBA transparency and are unchanged byte copies of the selected generated outputs. Scripts only read pixels for measurement and verification. No image was mirrored, recolored, cropped or background-keyed by script. `prompts.json`, `profile-hands-prompts.json` and `provenance.json` preserve exact prompts, selected sources and hashes. Opaque checkerboard extraction attempts were rejected.

Each of the four rider sheets contains sixteen separate parts: head, torso, hip cloth, cloak, left/right upper arms, forearms, hands, thighs, shins and boots. Each of the four horse sheets contains twenty parts: head, neck, barrel, tail; four upper legs; two foreleg lower sections; two hind shanks; two hind cannons; four hooves; mane; and saddle. The separate hind segments support stifle, hock and fetlock articulation. Rear saddle and hoof surfaces were generated explicitly. Duplicated feet on rider shins and duplicated hoof bulbs on horse cannons were removed during source correction, as were hanging stirrups baked into saddle drafts.

`src/roster-art/ashen-outrider.js` provides four-view descriptors, measured source bounds, arm/hand maps, source anchors and `MOUNT_PART_BINDINGS`. Bounds use connected alpha above 24, padded by two pixels. Limb attachment centers use alpha-weighted rows. Head poll/muzzle and neckRoot/poll anchors are placed explicitly on the source anatomy; the ear tip is not the poll. Barrel and saddle have distinct anchors. All sixteen hoof parts have measured `sole`, `heel` and `toe` anchors: alpha above 64 identifies the final painted row and the footprint across its lowest fifteen percent. The sole is at the bottom edge of the last painted row, with profile heel/toe ordered according to facing. Front/back heel and toe coincide at the sole center.

The profile hand supplement replaces the four original side-view hand cells without altering the base atlases. Its order is right-far-left-palm, right-near-right-dorsal, left-near-left-dorsal, left-far-right-palm. Both top thumbs point screen-right and both bottom thumbs point screen-left. Near hands show knuckles/tendons; far hands show palm creases and curled finger pads. All four supplement sockets use the centroids of substantial enclosed thumb-index alpha openings (2,131 to 3,681 pixels), rather than tiny gaps between fingers. Front hands also have enclosed alpha grip openings. Back hands have open C apertures measured within the visually inspected thumb/finger windows, documented separately in `measurement-report.json`. Front hand source rectangles are reordered anatomically without reflecting artwork. The renderer must map wrist and grip together.

Both profile torso shoulder sockets remain covered by opaque charcoal fabric under the moving sleeve, without a bare skin circle or transparent hole. All 148 supplied component rectangles and all nine RGBA source copies were checked. There are 144 active body/horse components after the four side-view hands are replaced. These source-art checks do not establish final player-size assembly, animation or gameplay acceptance; those remain part of the shared mounted renderer review.
