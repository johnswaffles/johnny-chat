# The Living Meadow

Release: `20260905-meadow1` · September 5, 2026

The flat, fine-grained ground now has an original painted meadow material, twelve layered plant silhouettes, traveling breezes and local foot/hoof response. Blades stay rooted, part around moving characters, and settle after they pass. ## Implementation

- Cached ground material and biome masks stay attached to world coordinates.
- Seeded visible chunks distribute varied clumps independently of match randomness. Dense woodland, foundations, cultivated fields, roads and resource bases suppress standing grass.
- Grass joins the existing entity depth order, allowing near blades to cover boots while structures hide plants behind them.
- Coherent wind moves the upper blades. Nearby passage curves three joined sections around a fixed root; horses disturb a wider area. Contact decays without changing paths or leaving permanent terrain marks.
- Both the atmosphere switch and reduced-motion preference stop ambient grass sway. Character contact remains tied to actual movement.
- Cached visible results and small source mips avoid rebuilding terrain during normal frames. Limits: 2,500 visible tufts, 2,048 cached chunks and 1,024 temporary contacts. Distant map views fade out individual tufts.
