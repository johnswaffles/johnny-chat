# Crown Scout rider and horse artwork

Eight original built-in image-generation sheets preserve the Crown Scout's chestnut horse, teal wool and old-gold trim, brown leather tack, and practical light cavalry identity. The rider has an open iron helmet, brown hair, ivory sleeves, leather vest and bracers, fingerless gloves, olive riding breeches and separate riding boots. His spear and small round shield remain runtime equipment; neither is baked into a character component.

Each PNG is 1024 by 1536 with genuine RGBA transparency. All selected generated files are copied unchanged into this folder; scripts only read pixels to measure bounds, transparency and attachment points. No image was mirrored, recolored, cropped or background-keyed by script. `prompts.json` preserves the exact generation, directional, corrective and alpha-extraction prompts. `provenance.json` records selected tool outputs and matching source/output hashes.

The four `rider-*.png` sheets each contain sixteen separate components: head, torso, hip cloth, cloak, left/right upper arms, forearms, hands, thighs, shins and boots. The four `horse-*.png` sheets each contain twenty: head, neck, barrel, tail; four upper legs; two front lower legs; two hind shanks; two hind cannons; four hooves; mane; saddle with saddlecloth. Horse anatomy therefore has separate stifle, hock and fetlock attachments. The saddle is separate from the breathing barrel. All camera views are independently generated surfaces, including profile hoof toes and rear saddle cantle.

`src/roster-art/crown-scout.js` contains the ready four-view descriptor, arm/hand frame maps, horse source rectangles and all twenty horse bindings. Source rectangles come from connected alpha above24, padded by two pixels. Limb roots/tips use alpha-weighted joint-center rows. Head poll/muzzle and neckRoot/poll anchors are explicitly placed on the source anatomy, rather than ear tips or bounding-box centers. Neck and mane source axes run bottom-to-top; head, tail and limbs use their stated anatomical attachment direction. Barrel anchors locate its center, saddle anchors locate its seat near the top of the cloth assembly.

Front hand rectangles are reordered into anatomical handedness without reflecting artwork. Those two relaxed grips have open apertures: their sockets use the measured transparent region bounded by thumb and fingers. The other six hands have enclosed transparent thumb/finger openings whose alpha centroids supply their sockets. `measurement-report.json` records the method, counts and values honestly for each source. The renderer must map both wrist and grip, not merely place the hand next to a weapon.

All144 component rectangles, all source alpha channels and byte-preserving copies were checked. The source views were inspected for camera direction, separate segments, cloth lining in shoulder sockets, absence of baked equipment and correct rear surfaces. Final player-size assembly, motion and gameplay acceptance belong to the shared mounted renderer review; this source-art check does not mark those complete.

Mounted floor correction adds explicit `sole`, `heel` and `toe` anchors to all sixteen hoof cutouts. The source floor is the lower edge of the last pixel row with alpha above64, and the profile footprint spans the opaque pixels in the lowest fifteen percent of source height. Front/back heel and toe coincide at the sole center. `hoof-contact-measurements.json` records the measured values. The mounted renderer maps the source fetlock and supporting sole to their projected anatomy and ground contact, keeping flat hooves on the floor and rotating pitch about the supporting edge. No PNG pixels changed. The permanent regression command is `node --test tools/mounted-render-regression.mjs`.

## Rider profile anatomy refinement

Two built-in image_gen supplements preserve the accepted rider and horse sheets. `profile-hands-v1.png` contains four independently authored fingerless-gloved hands with correct near dorsal/far palm surfaces and forward thumb edges; it retains the original 4.7 by 7.2 hand dimensions. `profile-torsos-v1.png` contains right and left profile torsos with continuous cream shirt underlays closing the former hollow shoulder sockets. The four body sheets, their original bounds and arm axes, front/back hands, horse art, mount bindings and sole anchors are unchanged. Profile torso part 1 redirects through the shared renderer override contract. Exact prompts, original output paths, alpha-measured bounds and unchanged-copy hashes are in the profile-supplement JSON records. Open glove grip cups use visually calibrated centers.
