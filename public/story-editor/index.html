About voice & privacy

Johnny’s voice is AI generated. Turning on voice sends microphone audio and selected story context to OpenAI. Read sends short pieces of saved text for narration. Writing requests use your manuscript context and can incur API charges. Your original stays saved; story notes belong only to this manuscript.
